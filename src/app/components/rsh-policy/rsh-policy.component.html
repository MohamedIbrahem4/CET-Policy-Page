Excursion Policy

Destination Egypt Travel

  1. Please ensure the date on the excursion ticket is correctly written
  2. Pick up time for all excursions takes place at the reception area of your hotel
  3. For pick-ups before 07:00 please arrange a wake-up call and breakfast one day in advance
  4. Please don’t leave valuables (camera, money, etc.) in the bus during sightseeing
  5. Bring passport copies, water, sunscreen, comfortable shoes, and a pillow for long journeys
  6. Evening excursions may get chilly – bring extra clothing
  7. For desert excursions and Aqua Park, a safety form must be read and signed
  8. Quad & safari excursions are not allowed for pregnant women or guests with spine problems
  9. Cancellation on the excursion day receives no refund
  10. Environmental fees apply to safari and boat trips
  11. Entrance to Tutankhamun’s tomb and pyramid chambers is exclusive

* Minimum 36 hours notice is required for full refund

* Flight or hotel excursions refunded after deducting costs

* Flight excursions receive 25% refund with a doctor’s note

* Overnight admin fees: £35 Cairo / £20 Luxor

* Cancellation 36–24 hours before: 50% refund

* Cancellation within 24 hours: no refund

* Ticket must be handed to RSH representative for any refund

* Minimum passengers required: 4

* Single traveler room supplement applies

* All ancient site tours include licensed English-speaking guides

* Child age: 2 to 11.99 years

* No refund for missed excursions

* Date changes allowed for short excursions only (24h before)

* Change fees: £6 short / £10 long excursions

* Excludes flight & overnight excursions

* Operated by Destination Egypt Travel – fully health & safety checked
